How to Use Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth in the first field.
  2. Optionally change the second date if you want your age on a specific day (defaults to today).
  3. Click Calculate Age.
  4. View your exact age in years, months, days, and total days lived.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Age Calculator free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up required.

Does it store my date of birth?

No. Everything runs in your browser — your data never leaves your device.

Can I calculate age as of a future or past date?

Yes, just change the second date field to any date you want.

🤖 Ask AI about this tool

Tap a question for an instant answer. Full conversational AI is coming soon.

How is age calculated in years, months and days?
We take the difference between your birth date and the target date, adjusting for varying month lengths so the years/months/days are always exact.
Why do I need a total days count?
It's useful for milestones, legal documents, or just curiosity about how many days you've been alive.
Can I use this for a pet's age or an event's age?
Yes — this calculator works for any two dates, not just human birthdays.
