What is a SIP?
A Systematic Investment Plan lets you invest a fixed amount regularly (usually monthly) into a mutual fund.
Why does starting early matter?
Longer durations let compounding work longer, often producing significantly higher maturity values.
Is 12% a realistic return?
It's a common assumption for equity mutual funds historically, but actual returns are never guaranteed.
