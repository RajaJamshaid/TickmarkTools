Password Generator

Generate strong, random passwords using your browser's secure crypto engine — nothing is sent to any server.

How to Use Password Generator

  1. Choose your desired password length using the slider.
  2. Select which character types to include.
  3. Click Generate Password.
  4. Copy the password using the Copy button.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

Are the passwords truly random?

Yes, we use your browser's built-in crypto.getRandomValues() for secure randomness.

Are generated passwords stored anywhere?

No, nothing is saved or transmitted — the password only exists on your screen.

What makes a password strong?
Length (12+ characters), a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols, and avoiding common words or patterns.
How long should my password be?
At least 12–16 characters is recommended for most accounts today.
Should I reuse passwords across sites?
No — use a unique password for every account, ideally managed with a password manager.
