How is the difference calculated?
We compute years, months, and days between the two calendar dates, then also give the raw total day count.
Why is total days useful?
It's helpful for contracts, deadlines, or simply understanding elapsed time in one consistent unit.
Does it account for leap years?
Yes, since it works directly with JavaScript Date objects which handle leap years correctly.
