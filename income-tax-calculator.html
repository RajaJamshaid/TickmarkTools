What is a progressive tax slab system?
Income is taxed in bands — each portion of income is taxed at the rate for that band, not your entire income at one rate.
What is 'effective tax rate'?
It's your total tax divided by your total income — usually lower than your top marginal slab rate.
Should I use this for official filing?
No — always use your country's official tax calculator or a licensed accountant for filing purposes.
