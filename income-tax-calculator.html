How progressive tax slabs work

Why your "tax rate" isn’t one flat number

Progressive taxation applies different rates to different portions (slabs) of your income — only the income within each bracket is taxed at that bracket’s rate, not your entire income. Your "effective rate" is your total tax divided by your total income, which is always lower than your top marginal bracket rate.

When to use this tool

Useful for understanding how progressive taxation works, comparing income scenarios, or getting a rough estimate before consulting a tax professional.

✓ Pros

  • Works instantly, right in your browser
  • Shows effective rate, not just top bracket rate
  • No sign-up, no ads blocking the result

✕ Limitations

  • Uses illustrative example slabs, not your country’s actual rates
  • Doesn’t include deductions, exemptions, or credits
  • Not a substitute for professional tax advice

Accuracy & limitations

This calculator is built for speed and accuracy, but it's worth understanding what it does and doesn't account for:

  • Illustrative slabs only. The tax brackets used here are examples — they do not represent any specific country’s actual current tax law.
  • No deductions modeled. Standard deductions, exemptions, and tax credits that could lower your actual taxable income aren’t included.
  • Not professional advice. For actual tax filing, consult your country’s official tax authority or a qualified tax professional.

Income Tax Calculator FAQ

Is this income tax calculator free to use?

Yes, the Income Tax Calculator is completely free with no sign-up required.

Does it store my income data?

No — all calculations happen locally in your browser, and nothing is saved.

Are these the actual tax slabs for my country?

No, these are illustrative example slabs — check your country’s official tax authority for accurate current brackets.

What is "effective tax rate"?

It’s your total tax divided by your total income, expressed as a percentage — always lower than your top marginal bracket rate.

Does this include deductions or exemptions?

No, this calculates tax on the taxable income you enter directly, without applying any deductions or exemptions.

Does this tool store or upload my data?

No. All calculations run entirely in your browser, and nothing you enter is ever sent to a server or stored.
