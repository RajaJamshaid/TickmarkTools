Tap a question for an instant answer. Full conversational AI is coming soon.

    
What's the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?
Title Case capitalizes most words (used in headlines); Sentence case only capitalizes the first word of each sentence.

    
When should I use UPPERCASE text?
Sparingly — for emphasis, acronyms, or short labels; large blocks of uppercase text are harder to read.

    
Can I convert code snippets with this?
It's designed for prose text; converting code case may break syntax like variable names.
