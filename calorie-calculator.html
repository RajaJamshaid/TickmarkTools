What is BMR?
Basal Metabolic Rate — the calories your body burns at complete rest just to maintain basic functions.
What is TDEE?
Total Daily Energy Expenditure — your BMR multiplied by an activity factor, giving your full daily maintenance calories.
How do I lose weight using this number?
Eating roughly 500 calories below your TDEE daily is a common approach for gradual, sustainable weight loss.
