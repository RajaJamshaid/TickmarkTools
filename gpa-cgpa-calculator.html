Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

What grade scale does this use?

Standard 4.0 scale (A=4, B=3, C=2, D=1, F=0), but you can enter any grade point value your institution uses.

What's the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA is typically for one term/semester; CGPA is the cumulative average across all terms.

How is GPA calculated?
GPA = sum of (grade points × credit hours) ÷ sum of credit hours.
What's a good GPA?
Generally, 3.5+ is considered excellent, 3.0-3.5 is good, though standards vary by institution.
How do I convert percentage to GPA?
Conversion scales vary by country/institution — check your school's official conversion table.
