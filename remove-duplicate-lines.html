Frequently Asked Questions

      

        
Is it free?

        
Yes.

      

      

        
Does the order of lines change?

        
No, the first occurrence of each unique line is kept in its original position.

      

      

        
What counts as a duplicate?

        
Lines with identical text; enable case-sensitive mode to treat 'Apple' and 'apple' as different.

      






  

    
🤖 Ask AI about this tool

    
Tap a question for an instant answer. Full conversational AI is coming soon.

    
What's this tool useful for?
Cleaning email lists, deduplicating keyword lists, or tidying up any line-based data.

    
Does it trim whitespace?
Lines are compared as entered; extra leading/trailing spaces could make otherwise-identical lines appear different.

    
Can it handle large lists?
Yes, though very large lists (tens of thousands of lines) may take a moment to process.
