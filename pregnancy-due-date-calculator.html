Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

How accurate is this estimate?

It's a standard obstetric estimate (280 days from LMP); actual delivery dates vary — always confirm with your doctor.

Does it store my data?

No, everything is calculated locally in your browser.

🤖 Ask AI about this tool

Tap a question for an instant answer. Full conversational AI is coming soon.

How is the due date calculated?
Using Naegele's rule: 280 days (40 weeks) are added to the first day of your last menstrual period.
Is my baby's due date exact?
No, it's an estimate — only about 5% of babies are born exactly on their due date.
What if I know my conception date instead?
You can approximate LMP as 2 weeks before your conception date for this calculator.
