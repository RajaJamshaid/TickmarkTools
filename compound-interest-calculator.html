What is compound interest?
Interest calculated on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest from previous periods — it grows faster than simple interest.
How does compounding frequency affect returns?
More frequent compounding (monthly vs yearly) results in slightly higher returns for the same nominal rate.
Is compound interest good for savings?
Yes, it's the core principle behind long-term investment growth — the earlier you start, the more it compounds.
