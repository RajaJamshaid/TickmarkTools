Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

Are rates live?

Yes, rates are fetched from a live public exchange-rate API at the time of conversion.

Does it work offline?

No, an internet connection is required to fetch current exchange rates.

🤖 Ask AI about this tool

Tap a question for an instant answer. Full conversational AI is coming soon.

How often do exchange rates update?
Rates typically update at least once a day from the underlying data provider.
Why do banks give a different rate?
Banks and money changers add their own margin on top of the market/mid-market rate shown here.
Can I convert any currency pair?
Yes, as long as both currencies are supported by the underlying exchange-rate data.
