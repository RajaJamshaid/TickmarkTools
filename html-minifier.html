🤖 Ask AI about this tool

    
Tap a question for an instant answer. Full conversational AI is coming soon.

    
Why minify HTML?
Smaller file sizes mean faster page loads, which improves user experience and SEO performance.

    
Is minified HTML harder to read?
Yes, minification removes formatting meant for humans — always keep an unminified source copy for editing.

    
Does minifying affect SEO directly?
Indirectly — faster load times (partly from minification) are a known ranking factor.
