Frequently Asked Questions

Why convert JPG to PNG?

PNG supports transparency and lossless quality, useful for logos and graphics.

Will file size increase?

Usually yes, since PNG is typically larger than JPG for photographic images.

What's the difference between JPG and PNG?
JPG uses lossy compression (smaller files, some quality loss); PNG is lossless and supports transparency.
When should I use PNG instead of JPG?
Use PNG for logos, icons, and graphics needing transparency or sharp edges; use JPG for photos.
Does converting JPG to PNG improve quality?
No — JPG's quality loss already happened during the original save; converting just changes the container format.
