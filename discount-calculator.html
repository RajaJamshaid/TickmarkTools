How to use the Discount Calculator

  1. Type in the original price of the item before any discount.

How Percentage Discounts Work

The math behind every sale price

A discount is simply a percentage taken off the original price. To find the final price, multiply the original price by the discount percentage to get the amount saved, then subtract that from the original price. This calculator does that instantly and precisely, so you never have to do the math in your head at checkout.

When to use this calculator

Use it while shopping in-store or online, when comparing sale prices across retailers, or when budgeting for a big purchase and you want to know the exact amount a discount will save you before you buy.

Calculating Stacked or Multiple Discounts

When more than one discount applies

Stores often apply more than one discount — a storewide sale plus a coupon, for example. Discounts stack multiplicatively, not by simply adding the percentages together. To calculate a stacked discount, run this calculator once with the original price and the first discount, then run it again using the resulting sale price as the new "original price" with the second discount applied. A 20% discount followed by a 10% discount is not the same as a single 30% discount — it works out to about 28% off overall.

Finding the Original Price From a Sale Price

Sometimes you know the sale price and the discount percentage but want to work backward to find the original price. The formula is: original price = sale price ÷ (1 − discount% / 100). For example, if an item costs $75 after a 25% discount, the original price was $75 ÷ 0.75 = $100. This calculator is built for the forward direction (original price → sale price), but the reverse formula above lets you solve it by hand in seconds when needed.

Accuracy & limitations

This calculator is built for speed and accuracy, but it's worth understanding what it does and doesn't account for:

  • Single discount at a time. For stacked or multiple discounts, apply the calculator sequentially rather than entering combined percentages.
  • No tax included. Results reflect a pure percentage discount and don't add sales tax, VAT, or GST — check your final receipt separately.
  • Rounding to the cent. Results are rounded to two decimal places, which can occasionally differ by a fraction of a cent from a retailer's own rounding rules.

Discount Calculator FAQ

Is this discount calculator free to use?

Yes, the Discount Calculator is completely free with no sign-up required.

Does it handle multiple or stacked discounts?

It calculates one discount at a time. For stacked discounts, run the calculator again using the resulting sale price as the new original price.

Is this accurate for any currency?

Yes. Since the calculation is a pure percentage, it works correctly for any currency — dollars, euros, rupees, or anything else.

How do I calculate a discounted price manually?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage to find the amount saved, then subtract that amount from the original price.

Can I find the original price before a discount?

Yes — divide the sale price by (1 minus the discount as a decimal). For example, a $75 sale price at 25% off means the original price was $100.

Does this tool store or upload my data?

No. All calculations run entirely in your browser, and nothing you enter is ever sent to a server or stored.
