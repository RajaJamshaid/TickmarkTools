Does it handle multiple discounts?

Is this accurate for any currency?

How do I calculate a discounted price?
Final price = Original price × (1 − discount% / 100).
How do I calculate two stacked discounts?
Apply the first discount, then apply the second discount to the resulting price — not the original.
What if I know the final price and want the discount %?
Discount % = (1 − final price / original price) × 100.
