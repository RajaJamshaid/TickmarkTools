How to Use Image Cropper

  1. Upload an image.
  2. Click and drag on the image to select the crop area.
  3. Click Crop Selected Area.
  4. Download your cropped image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

Can I redo my selection?

Yes, just drag a new selection box before clicking crop.

What format is the cropped output?

PNG, preserving transparency if present.

🤖 Ask AI about this tool

Tap a question for an instant answer. Full conversational AI is coming soon.

How do I select a precise crop area?
Click and drag from one corner of the desired area to the opposite corner — release to set the selection.
Can I crop to a specific aspect ratio?
This tool uses freeform selection; drag carefully to approximate your desired ratio.
Will cropping reduce image quality?
No, cropping just removes the outer area — remaining pixels stay at original quality.
