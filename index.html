FAQ

Common questions

Is TickmarkTools really free?

Yes. Every tool on TickmarkTools is free to use, with no hidden limits, no forced trial, and no credit card required.

Do my files ever leave my device?

Most tools — including all PDF, image, and text tools — run entirely in your browser. Your files are processed locally and are never uploaded to a server.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can use every tool on TickmarkTools instantly, with no sign-up and no account required.

Can I use TickmarkTools on my phone?

Yes. Every tool is built mobile-first and works the same way on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Is there a file size or usage limit?

There's no artificial limit. Since most processing happens in your own browser, the practical limit depends on your device's memory rather than any restriction we impose.

Can businesses use TickmarkTools tools?

Yes, tools are free for personal and professional use alike. See our Terms of Service for full usage details.
