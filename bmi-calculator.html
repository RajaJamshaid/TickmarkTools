Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

Does it store my health data?

No, calculations happen entirely in your browser.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI is a general screening tool and may not account for muscle mass, age, or body composition — consult a doctor for personalized advice.

How do I calculate BMI?
BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². This tool does that calculation for you automatically.
What is a healthy BMI?
A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is generally considered a normal healthy range for adults.
Is BMI different for men and women?
The BMI formula is the same, but healthy ranges can vary slightly based on age, muscle mass, and other factors.
