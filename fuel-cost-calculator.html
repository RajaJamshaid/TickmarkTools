Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

Does it use live fuel prices?

No, enter your local fuel price manually for accuracy.

Can I use miles and gallons?

Enter equivalent values — the formula works the same regardless of units, just be consistent.

How is fuel cost calculated?
Liters needed = distance ÷ mileage. Total cost = liters needed × price per liter.
How can I improve my mileage?
Maintain steady speeds, keep tires properly inflated, and avoid excessive idling.
Does this account for traffic or terrain?
No, it's a straightforward estimate based on average mileage.
