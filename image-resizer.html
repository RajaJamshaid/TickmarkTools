How to Use Image Resizer

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

Will resizing distort my image?

Not if you keep aspect ratio locked — otherwise the image will stretch to your exact dimensions.

What format is the output?

PNG, preserving transparency if present.

What size should I resize images for a website?
Common web sizes are 1200px wide for hero images and 600-800px for content images, but it depends on your layout.
Will resizing reduce file size too?
Usually yes, since smaller dimensions generally mean less image data — use the Image Compressor for further reduction.
Can I upscale a small image?
Yes, but enlarging beyond the original resolution may reduce sharpness.
