Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

Will formatting and images be preserved?

This browser-based converter focuses on accurate text extraction; complex layouts, images, and precise formatting are not preserved.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No — the conversion happens locally in your browser.

🤖 Ask AI about this tool

Tap a question for an instant answer. Full conversational AI is coming soon.

Why isn't my formatting preserved?
Browser-based conversion focuses on extracting readable text safely, without a server; complex layouts require heavier server-side processing.
Can I convert scanned (image-only) PDFs?
Not with this tool — it extracts embedded text, so scanned PDFs would need OCR first.
Is the output a real .docx file?
Yes, it's a genuine Word-compatible .docx file you can open and edit in Microsoft Word or Google Docs.
