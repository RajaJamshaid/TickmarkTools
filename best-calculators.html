Best Free Online Calculators for Everyday Life

From age to EMI to BMI — the most useful free calculators, organized by what you're trying to solve.

Calculators are the most-searched category of online tool for a reason — everyone needs one eventually, for something. Here's a roundup of the ones people reach for most, and exactly when to use each.

Everyday Life

  • Age Calculator — exact age in years, months, and days from a birth date.
  • Date Difference Calculator — days between any two dates.
  • Time Calculator — add or subtract hours, minutes, and seconds.

Health

  • BMI Calculator — Body Mass Index and health category from height and weight.
  • Calorie Calculator — estimated daily calorie needs.
  • Pregnancy Due Date Calculator — estimated due date from your last period.

Money & Finance

  • EMI / Loan Calculator — monthly loan installment amounts.
  • Mortgage Calculator — estimated home loan payments.
  • Compound Interest Calculator — how investments grow over time.
  • SIP Investment Calculator — future value of monthly investments.
  • Retirement Calculator — projected retirement savings.

Shopping & Bills

  • Discount Calculator — final price after a percentage discount.
  • GST / VAT Calculator — tax-inclusive or exclusive pricing.
  • Fuel Cost Calculator — estimated trip fuel cost.
  • Electricity Bill Calculator — estimated monthly electricity bill.
