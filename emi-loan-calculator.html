Does it store my loan data?

No, all math runs locally in your browser.

What formula is used?

The standard reducing-balance EMI formula: EMI = P×r×(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1).

How does EMI work?
Each month you pay a fixed amount that covers part interest and part principal, with interest reducing over time as the balance shrinks.
What lowers my EMI?
A longer tenure or a lower interest rate reduces your monthly EMI, though a longer tenure increases total interest paid.
Is prepayment worth it?
Prepaying reduces the principal early, which can significantly cut total interest over the loan term.
