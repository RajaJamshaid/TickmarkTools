Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

Is this projection guaranteed?

No, it's an estimate based on your inputs — actual investment returns vary and aren't guaranteed.

Does it account for inflation?

No, this is a nominal (non-inflation-adjusted) projection.

How is retirement savings projected?
We compound your current savings plus monthly contributions at your expected annual return rate until your retirement age.
What's a realistic expected return rate?
Historically, diversified stock market investments have averaged 7-10% annually over the long term, though this varies by market and risk level.
Should I account for inflation?
Yes, for real purchasing power you should reduce your expected return by the inflation rate — this calculator shows nominal values.
