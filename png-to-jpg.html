Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

What happens to transparent areas?

JPG doesn't support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white.

Will quality decrease?

JPG uses lossy compression, so there may be minor quality loss compared to PNG.

Why convert PNG to JPG?
JPG files are usually much smaller, making them faster to load and easier to share, especially for photos.
Will I lose transparency?
Yes, JPG doesn't support transparency — transparent pixels convert to white.
Is JPG good for logos?
Not usually — PNG is better for logos with transparency or sharp edges; JPG suits photographic images.
