How do I find X% of Y?
Multiply Y by (X divided by 100). For example, 20% of 500 is 500 × 0.20 = 100.
How do I calculate percentage increase or decrease?
Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, then multiply by 100.
What's the difference between percentage and percentage points?
A percentage point is the arithmetic difference between two percentages, while a percentage is a relative measure.
