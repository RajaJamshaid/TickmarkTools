Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

Does this include taxes and insurance?

No, this estimates principal and interest only.

Is my data stored?

No, everything is calculated locally in your browser.

🤖 Ask AI about this tool

Tap a question for an instant answer. Full conversational AI is coming soon.

How is my monthly payment calculated?
We use the standard amortization formula on the loan amount (price minus down payment) over your chosen term.
Does a bigger down payment help?
Yes — it reduces your loan amount, monthly payment, and total interest paid.
What term length is best?
Shorter terms have higher monthly payments but far less total interest; longer terms lower monthly payments but cost more overall.
