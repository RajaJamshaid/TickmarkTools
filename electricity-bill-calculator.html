How to Use Electricity Bill Calculator

  1. Enter total units (kWh) consumed for the billing period.
  2. Enter your utility's rate per unit.
  3. Optionally add fixed monthly charges.
  4. Click Calculate Bill.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

Does this include taxes or slabs?

This is a simplified flat-rate estimate; real bills may include slabs, taxes, and surcharges that vary by provider.

Where do I find my units consumed?

Check your previous electricity bill or meter readings.

How is my electricity bill calculated?
Total bill = (units consumed × rate per unit) + any fixed charges.
How can I reduce my electricity bill?
Use energy-efficient appliances, unplug unused devices, and monitor high-consumption appliances like ACs and heaters.
Why does my actual bill differ from this estimate?
Real bills often use tiered/slab rates and additional taxes not captured in this simplified calculator.
