Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Does it store my text?

No, everything is processed locally in your browser.

Is punctuation counted?

🤖 Ask AI about this tool

Tap a question for an instant answer. Full conversational AI is coming soon.

Why do character limits matter?
Platforms like Twitter/X, meta descriptions, and SMS messages enforce character limits for display and technical reasons.
What's a typical meta description length?
Around 150-160 characters is the commonly recommended limit for search engine snippets.
Does this count emojis correctly?
Most emojis are counted as characters, though some complex emoji sequences may count as multiple characters.
