What's the difference between JPG, PNG and WebP?
JPG suits photos with smaller file sizes; PNG supports transparency and lossless quality; WebP often offers the best balance of size and quality.
Which format should I choose for a logo?
PNG or WebP, since both support transparency needed for logos on colored backgrounds.
Is WebP supported everywhere?
Yes, all modern browsers support WebP, though very old browsers may not.
